It covers arbitrary orientations, boundary contact, and disjoint interiors; the verification run accepted all 7,920 local Lean modules with zero admissions. A substantial portion of the discussion focuses on how the proof actually works and what the tooling represents. These branches are then pruned by propagating overlap constraints and solving packing linear programs before subdividing residual spaces. In their view, the core techniques have been known since the late 1980s; machine learning didn't perform superhuman mathematics, but rather lowered the barrier enough for enthusiastic amateurs to formally verify combinatorial cases too massive to check by hand.
